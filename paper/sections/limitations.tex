\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The framework is intentionally diagnostic, and it needs more environments before it should be treated as a general predictor. ALFWorld and WebShop provide the main controlled comparison, while SQL is additional evidence for a specific boundary case: regular action syntax with narrow semantic transfer radius. This is stronger than a two-point anecdote, but it is not yet a full map of the space.

We also evaluate a limited set of base-model conditions, and most memory variants use single-trajectory evaluation without repeated-run confidence estimates. Different LLMs may vary in how strongly they follow retrieved memories, how well they discount irrelevant context, and how often they recover from near-miss advice. These model interactions are part of the phenomenon rather than a nuisance variable, and future work should measure them directly.

The next high-value test is ScienceWorld~\citep{wang2022scienceworld}. It is an orthogonal environment to ALFWorld and WebShop: scientific tasks should provide compositional procedures and reusable causal knowledge, but with different objects, dynamics, and failure modes from household manipulation. We have not run or reported ScienceWorld results here. Its role is future validation of the proposed transfer conditions, especially whether broad learning transfer radius predicts memory benefit outside ALFWorld.
